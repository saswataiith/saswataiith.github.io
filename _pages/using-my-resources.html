TEACHING AND RESEARCH

Using my resources

If you use my notes, codes, figures or interactive demonstrations in your teaching, research or presentations, please acknowledge the source and include a link to the relevant page on this website.

Unless a resource states otherwise, the original teaching text, notes and figures that I own on this website are available under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0). You may copy, share and adapt this material, including for commercial use. Credit me, link to the source and the licence, and indicate any changes you make.

For example: “Adapted from [resource title] by Saswata Bhattacharyya, [page link], CC BY 4.0. Changes: [brief description].”

Ternary spinodal code: I require my explicit written permission for academic or commercial use, including teaching, research, adaptation or redistribution. Citation alone does not give permission. Read the code-use terms and request permission at saswata_at_msme.iith.ac.in. Earlier MIT releases retain their existing permissions.

My other original Julia, Python/NumPy and C source is available under the MIT licence where marked. You may use, modify and redistribute it, including commercially; retain the copyright and licence notice. See the website code licence scope and the licence supplied with each repository. The elastic laboratory and elastic solvers keep their existing GPL licences. Libraries and code credited to others keep their own terms. The Creative Commons licence above does not apply to software.
