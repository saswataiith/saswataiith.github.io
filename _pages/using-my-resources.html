TEACHING AND RESEARCH

Using my resources

If you use my notes, codes, figures or interactive demonstrations in your teaching, research or presentations, please acknowledge the source and include a link to the relevant page on this website.

Software follows the licence supplied with each code or repository. Where a code has no licence, please contact me before redistributing or modifying it.

Published papers, students’ thesis material, jointly owned material, institute logos and other material credited to others keep their existing copyright and licence terms. Follow those terms, cite the original paper where applicable, and retain the authors’ names.
