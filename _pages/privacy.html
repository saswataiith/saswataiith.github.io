WEBSITE

Privacy and visitor statistics

I use Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure visits, page views, referral sources and page performance. It does not use cookies, browser storage or fingerprinting to identify visitors. There is no analytics consent banner.

The statistics are aggregate measurements, rather than a record of an identified person's browsing history. I do not send names, email addresses or information entered into teaching tools to this service. Read Cloudflare's explanation of Web Analytics and its privacy policy.

Google Analytics has been removed. If you previously accepted it, its old cookies may remain until they expire; they are no longer used by this website. You can remove them through your browser settings.

GitHub hosts this website and processes requests under its own privacy statement. Other services used for fonts and embedded content may receive requests when those resources load.
