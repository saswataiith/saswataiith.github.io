Privacy and visitor statistics

I use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use this academic website, including which pages they visit and how they arrive here. Analytics starts only if you select “Accept analytics”. Declining does not prevent you from using any page or interactive demonstration.

Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information about page visits, browser and device characteristics, and approximate location. I do not send names, email addresses or information entered into teaching tools to Analytics. Advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled in the website tag.

Your choice is stored in this browser. You can change it below. If you withdraw consent after accepting, reload the page to stop the Analytics script already running on that page. Existing Analytics cookies can be removed through your browser settings.
