Privacy and visitor statistics

Google Analytics has been disabled on this website. The website no longer loads its tracking script or displays an analytics consent banner. Cookie-free visitor statistics are being set up; visitor statistics are currently disabled.

If you previously accepted Google Analytics, cookies already stored in your browser may remain until they expire. You can remove them through your browser settings. They are no longer used by this website.

GitHub hosts this website and processes requests under its own privacy statement. Other services used for fonts and embedded content may receive requests when those resources load.
