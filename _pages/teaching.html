MY NPTEL COURSE

Thermodynamics and
Kinetics of Materials.

My NPTEL course introduces thermodynamics and kinetics for students of metallurgy and materials engineering: thermodynamic potentials, phase equilibria, solutions, chemical reactions, surfaces, reaction rates and diffusion in solids.

A virtual laboratory connects these concepts with calculations of phase equilibria and diffusion using Thermo-Calc’s education package, pycalphad and FiPy.

TRANSPORT PHENOMENA

See the fields.
Understand the transport.

My Transport Phenomena teaching connects physical intuition with field and property tensors, gradient, divergence and curl, and the roles of symmetry through the Curie and Neumann principles.

The accompanying TensorLab is an interactive book with visual demonstrations and explorations that students can use alongside the course notes. The reading framework draws on the following books:

  • J. F. Nye, Physical Properties of Crystals: Their Representation by Tensors and Matrices — Oxford University Press.
  • Robert E. Newnham, Properties of Materials: Anisotropy, Symmetry, Structure — Oxford University Press.
  • R. Byron Bird, Warren E. Stewart and Edwin N. Lightfoot, Transport Phenomena — Wiley.
  • James R. Welty, Charles E. Wicks, Robert E. Wilson and Gregory L. Rorrer, Fundamentals of Momentum, Heat, and Mass Transfer, 5th edition — Wiley.
