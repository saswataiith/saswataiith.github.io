TRANSPORT PHENOMENA

See the fields.
Understand the transport.

My Transport Phenomena teaching begins with forces and potentials, develops conservation from finite control volumes to local differential equations, and then connects fluxes with gradients, field tensors, property tensors and material symmetry.

The same structure appears repeatedly

\[\text{potential or state}\ \longrightarrow\ \text{driving force}\ \longrightarrow\ \text{flux}\ \longrightarrow\ \nabla\!\cdot(\text{flux})\ \longrightarrow\ \text{local accumulation}.\]

A potential gradient supplies a driving force, a constitutive relation connects that force with a flux, and the divergence of the flux enters the local conservation law. The details differ for momentum, heat, mass, charge and microstructural evolution, but this reasoning pattern remains recognizable.
