THE PEOPLE BEHIND THE CURIOSITY

How SHAP sparked
my interest in AI/ML.

My wife, Joyita, is an experimentalist who loves to experiment and explore. Having worked on thermoelectrics, she began exploring a thermoelectrics database out of curiosity, using machine learning and SHAP to understand the patterns it contained. She was also looking for patterns in microstructures.

While I was focused on phase-field modeling, she would show me what she was finding. I found the results very informative and began paying closer attention to what AI and machine learning could help us understand. Her curiosity and experiments sparked my own interest. She is the inspiration behind my journey into AI/ML, and I owe this learning to her.

She shares her own explorations, tutorials and examples on Materials Data Explorer ↗.
