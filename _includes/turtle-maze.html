A TURTLE’S TEST

Can I get there without leaving my phase?

Our turtle can walk only on the pale paths. Follow it from START to the carrot at END. The purple gates join opposite edges: out on the right, back in on the left!

Ready? A connected path lets us travel without crossing a wall.

The green walls represent the other phase. The little island is disconnected from our path. Reaching the carrot proves connectivity between these two points. It does not establish that the whole phase is connected, or that both phases are bicontinuous. Crossing a seam alone is not proof of a noncontractible wrapping loop; that requires a path returning to a periodic copy of its starting point.
